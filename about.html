About Moonlytix

Your trusted analytics partner.

Moonlytix is a data and analytics consulting firm based in Dallas-Fort Worth. We work alongside operations and leadership teams at growing and mid-sized companies, starting with supply chain, where better data has a direct impact on cost, inventory, and service.

Our story

Built by people who lived the problem.

Our founders spent years inside daily operations, surrounded by manual work and scattered data, where decisions were still made on intuition instead of information. Reports took days to pull together, metrics meant something different depending on who you asked, and leaders still struggled to answer basic questions about efficiency, profitability, and growth.


Moonlytix was created to close that gap: to bring our knowledge of data and analytics into daily operations and help businesses make decisions they can actually trust, without the cost of a large consulting firm, and with everything built so your team fully owns it.


We are starting with supply chain and operations analytics, where our expertise runs deepest today. As we grow, we plan to bring the same approach to CX analytics, marketing, and finance.

Our mission

To illuminate the decisions that matter. We take the manual work and scattered data out of daily operations and turn them into insight your team can act on with confidence, starting with supply chain and operations.

Our vision

To become the analytics partner growing companies turn to first, helping leaders move from intuition to data-driven decisions across operations, CX, marketing, and finance.
