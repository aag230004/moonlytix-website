About Moonlytix

We exist because most operations teams aren’t short on data — they’re short on clarity.

Moonlytix is a supply chain and operations analytics consultancy for growing and mid-sized companies. We take the data you already collect and turn it into forecasts, dashboards, and decisions your team can run the business on.

Why we do this

Spreadsheets get you far. They don’t get you confident.

Most operations teams we meet aren’t lacking effort or intelligence — they’re lacking a system. Decisions get made from whichever spreadsheet was updated most recently, forecasts get built from memory, and by the time a report is ready, the moment it was meant to inform has already passed.


We build the analytics layer that closes that gap: grounded in how your operation actually runs, not a generic dashboard template. The goal isn’t a one-time report — it’s a way of seeing your business that your team keeps after we’re gone.

How we work

A structured path, not a black box.

01

Discover

We start by mapping what you actually have — systems, spreadsheets, data quality, and the gaps between them. No model gets built until we understand how information really moves through your operation.

02

Model

We build forecasting, inventory, supplier, and cost models tuned to your product mix, seasonality, and constraints — not an off-the-shelf template that ignores how your business is different.

03

Decide

Models become dashboards and decision frameworks written in plain language, so your leadership team can act on them immediately — without needing us in the room to interpret the numbers.

04

Operationalize

We embed the tooling into your team’s actual workflow and train the people who’ll own it, so the system keeps working — and keeps improving — long after the engagement ends.

What guides the work

Four things we don’t compromise on.

PRINCIPLE

Operations-first, not dashboards-first

We start with how decisions actually get made on your floor and in your planning meetings — not with a tool we’re trying to sell you.

PRINCIPLE

Right-sized for mid-market

You get senior-level analytics thinking without the enterprise price tag, timeline, or headcount required to run it.

PRINCIPLE

We stay until it’s operational

An analysis that lives in a slide deck doesn’t count. An engagement isn’t finished until your team can run it themselves.

PRINCIPLE

Plain-English reporting

Every dashboard and model has to be explainable to the person making the decision — not just the person who built it.
