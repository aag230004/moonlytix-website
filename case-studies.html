Case study 01

Order fulfillment performance for a distribution center

Data Integration Dashboards
01

The challenge

Fulfillment data lived inside the WMS, so seeing performance meant exporting reports by hand and stitching them together in spreadsheets.

02

What we did

We connected and cleaned the WMS data, then built an interactive Tableau dashboard the whole team can trust.

03

What you can see

Orders, revenue, units sold, and discounts, broken down by product, fulfillment center, and location, trended against last year.

This dashboard is interactive and works best on a larger screen. Open it in Tableau to explore every chart and filter.
