What we do

How we bring structure to your operations.

Data Integration & Warehousing

We design and build reliable data pipelines and integrations, including ERP systems like JD Edwards and SAP S/4HANA, WMS, TMS, and spreadsheets, into a data lake or warehouse for reporting, analytics, and decision-making.

Learn more

Supply Chain Dashboards

We create executive and operational dashboards in Tableau and Power BI that give teams clear visibility into inventory, forecasting, suppliers, transportation, productivity, and supply chain performance.

Network & Footprint Strategy

We analyze supply chain networks to identify opportunities to improve facility flows, transportation routes, service levels, cost efficiency, and operational performance.

Learn more

Automation & AI Agents
